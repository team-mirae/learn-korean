    Learn to type Korean

    You learn by typing: every answer is keys, checked on the real keyboard engine. First, pick the Korean layout you're aiming for. You can switch any time; each keeps its own progress.

    1. Type a word, then Space. Above each word are its standard romanization and the keys to press; turn either off to test yourself. The next key lights up on the keyboard.
    2. Several readings? The first shows as you type; Tab or ↓ moves to the next one in the list, then Space commits it.
    3. Everything runs in your browser, and your progress stays there.
